# Sort the structure

Check that parts, chapters and headings came through as the book has them, and fix what the import got wrong. Chapter markers in the audiobook, spoken titles and pauses all follow this structure, so sort it before the later steps.

## How

1. Choose **Check structure** to open the Book page. The tree on the left lists every volume, part and chapter. Look for a book that came in as one chapter, chapters that are far too long or short, a heading such as “Chapter 12” left as an ordinary paragraph, and missing or wrong titles. An untitled node shows as “Untitled chapter 3”.
2. Fix a few problems by hand from a node's menu (the ⋮ beside it in the tree or on its heading):
   - **Split chapter** on a paragraph starts a new chapter there. **Split part** and **Split volume** do the same one level up.
   - **Merge with previous** and **Merge with next** fold two neighbours together.
   - **Insert chapter after** and its siblings add an empty node. **Delete chapter** removes one with all it holds.
   - **Edit title** renames a volume, part or chapter.
3. When the chapter headings are plain lines in the text, use **Reread with split rules…** instead. Say whether the book has volumes and parts. Then, for each level, choose how a heading line looks: **Text prefix** (for example “Chapter”), **Number (Arabic)** or **Roman numerals**. The matching line becomes the title. This reread deletes attribution, audio, titles, pauses and your confirmed steps, so do it first.

## Done when

The tree matches the book. Choose **Confirm** on the Structure row, or **Skip** when the import was already right. Splitting, merging, inserting or deleting a chapter-level node later marks the step as changed, so you can confirm it again.
